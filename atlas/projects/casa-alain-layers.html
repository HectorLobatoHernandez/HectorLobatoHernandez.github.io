Build, open and explore

Scroll outside the viewer to construct and open the layers. Drag directly on the model to orbit; scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan. On mobile, use one finger to orbit and two to zoom or pan.

Double-click a component to frame its layer. Camera controls include orbit, zoom, plan view and automatic rotation. Capture 4K downloads the current 3D view at 3840 × 2160 pixels.

Use the checkboxes to hide layers. Isolate shows one layer with the floor. Selecting a layer or component updates the gallery with related interiors.

Cinema interior and DJ interior place the camera inside the space and remove roofs and walls for inspection. Overview restores the guided route.

Use the arrows to browse images, and the wheel or +/− to zoom. Actual pixels shows native resolution. New model views are rendered in 4K; original 800 × 450 images are labelled with their own resolution.

Geometry comes from the SketchUp source. Layers organise the presentation and do not certify installed equipment or the actual construction sequence. The model and renders may show different revisions.
