Casa Alain

The city as a portfolio

The city is built in daylight. Once construction finishes, sunset brings the night city to life. Day → night restores this sequence; Day / Night locks either view. Pause, replay or scrub the timeline to inspect the street plan, foundations, structure and envelopes.

Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. On mobile, use one finger to orbit and two to zoom or pan. Tap a plot to reveal its name; tap again to enter.

Point at a plot to reveal a light projection with its project name above the structure. Select it to approach the plan and watch the source model rise. Open layers, orbit, explore interiors and view the project references. The confidential concept remains a reserved plot in this public edition.

Night uses warm 2300 K presentation lighting, with a moving moon, street lights and animated tube neons. Fictional restaurants, cafés, shops and clubs occupy building ground floors. Explore the waterfront, terraces, backstreets and music quarter using the district buttons. City life pauses cars and pedestrians; Pause atmosphere stops ambient animation. Traffic is a local simulation with spacing, braking and signals. Open buildings removes roofs and makes front walls transparent to reveal fictional residents. The seven project miniatures simplify source geometry. 4K photo exports the city view. This conceptual navigation city does not represent real project locations.
