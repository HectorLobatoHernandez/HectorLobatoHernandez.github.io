Scope

An operational story, built as an independent prototype

GAZA Operations connects farm collection, quality gates, industrial production, pallets, storage, loading docks and outbound routes in one explorable experience. It is a personal demonstrator developed by Héctor Lobato Hernández for his application to the Dispatch and Logistics Manager position, already submitted through InfoJobs.

Transferable experience

Project management, planning, procurement, suppliers, costs, documentation, systems integration and incident handling.

Evidence boundary

The functional models and scenarios are based on public references and assumptions. There is no authorised internal telemetry, verified stock, employee data or live GPS fleet feed.

Responsible deployment

What an actual pilot would require

Discovery

Validate operational needs, floor plans, process owners, existing systems, data access and baseline KPIs with GAZA.

Read-only integration

Connect authorised ERP/MES/WMS/QA sources with IT/OT separation, access controls and source traceability.

Limited pilot

Test one dispatch flow with the team and compare measurable results with the baseline.

Evaluation

Expand only after operational acceptance, maintenance, security and cost have been checked.
