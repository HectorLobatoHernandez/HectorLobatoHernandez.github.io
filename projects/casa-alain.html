02 / DESIGN BRIEF

Cinema first. Music capable.

The acoustic report defines a private cinema optimized for cinematic listening and occasional DJ-set use, with the objective of achieving a faithful, neutral sound field without temporal or modal artefacts that degrade the listening experience.

CINEMA

Immersive architectural audio

Concealed loudspeakers, dedicated LCR, surround and overhead channels are integrated with the room architecture, screen wall and acoustic treatment.

  • Architectural in-wall / in-ceiling integration
  • Large-format cinema wall
  • Subwoofer integration
  • AVR configuration and calibration
DJ / CRITICAL LISTENING

Second listening position

The room is also developed around an occasional DJ listening position, which requires its own reflection-control strategy rather than treating the room as a single-seat cinema only.

  • DJ booth listening position
  • Local absorptive treatment
  • Compact parametric elements
  • Variable acoustic curtains

04 / ACOUSTIC ENGINEERING

Treatment by function.

The room is not treated from a decorative catalogue. Each treatment is positioned according to low-frequency behaviour, reflection paths, listening positions, temporal control and architectural constraints.

LOW FREQUENCY

6 bass traps

Six high-performance low-frequency units distributed across the front and rear corners to control modal build-up below approximately 125 Hz.

REAR WALL

Parametric hybrid surface

Full-surface rear treatment combining diffusion and absorption to improve temporal coherence and reduce interfering early reflections.

SIDE WALLS

4 QRD + 2 broadband

Four QRD diffusers address mid/high-frequency reflection behaviour while two broadband panels reduce primary reflections without over-damping.

DJ POSITION

Local reflection control

Compact parametric treatment and broadband absorption are positioned around the DJ listening area as a dedicated secondary listening condition.

SCREEN WALL

98-inch cinema wall

Absorptive treatment is placed below the large cinema display to reduce unwanted vertical reflection toward the listening area.

CEILING

5 diffusers + 8 clouds

Quadratic diffusers and broadband ceiling absorbers control flutter and early ceiling reflections while retaining spatial perception.

OPENINGS

Variable acoustic curtains

High-density curtains are proposed over the three openings so absorption and isolation behaviour can be adjusted between cinema and music use.

CONSTRUCTION

Ceiling + impact layer strategy

The project development also considers resilient ceiling build-up, high-mass layers, acoustic plasterboard and an impact-control layer below the final floor finish.

BESPOKE

Calculated in-situ design

Where standard elements are insufficient, treatment geometry is developed specifically for the room from modal behaviour, reflection control and target response.
