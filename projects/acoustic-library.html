ACOUSTIC SYSTEMS LIBRARY

Calculated for the room.

This is not a product catalogue. It is a design library of acoustic functions and construction strategies used as starting points for project-specific engineering. Geometry, quantity, placement, depth and material are adapted to the measured or modelled behaviour of each room.

01 / ABSORPTION

Broadband wall systems

For early reflections, reverberation control and large-area absorption.

  • Direct wall panels
  • Air-gap mounting
  • Fabric / timber / architectural finishes
  • Custom dimensions
02 / DIFFUSION

QRD / 2D / 3D diffusion

Mathematical and geometric diffusion for temporal and spatial control without over-absorption.

  • QRD sequences
  • 2D / 3D diffusers
  • Parametric surfaces
  • Custom scattering geometry
03 / LOW FREQUENCY

Bass-control systems

Corner, boundary and free-standing systems for modal energy and low-frequency decay control.

  • Corner traps
  • Deep porous absorption
  • Free-standing LF elements
  • Project-specific tuned solutions
04 / CEILINGS

Clouds / baffles / modular ceilings

Suspended acoustic systems that coordinate with lighting, HVAC and architectural ceiling design.

  • Cloud absorbers
  • Linear baffles
  • Modular ceiling fields
  • Integrated technical services
05 / VARIABLE ACOUSTICS

Curtains / movable elements

Deployable acoustic surfaces allow a room to change between listening, cinema, speech or music conditions.

  • Heavy acoustic curtains
  • Sliding absorbers
  • Movable screens
  • Multi-mode room strategies
06 / ARCHITECTURAL ACOUSTICS

Integrated surfaces

Acoustic performance can be embedded in walls, timber slats, perforated surfaces, reliefs and furniture.

  • Timber slat systems
  • Grooved / perforated panels
  • Decorative acoustic walls
  • Acoustic furniture
07 / SOUND ISOLATION

Mass / decoupling / sealing

Wall, floor and ceiling assemblies are developed from target isolation, source spectrum and structural transmission paths.

  • Resilient hangers / channels
  • High-mass membranes
  • Mineral wool cavities
  • Floating / impact-control layers
08 / BESPOKE

In-situ acoustic design

When a catalogue element is not the right answer, Studio Ingenia develops the geometry and construction specifically for the room.

  • Room modes
  • Reflection paths
  • Target RT / decay
  • Architectural constraints
