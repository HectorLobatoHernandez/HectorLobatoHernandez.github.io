# MASTER BRIEF — Sound Club, Palma

## Public identity
Sound Club, Palma

## Location
Puerto Deportivo de Palma

## Project nature
Hospitality project integrating professional audio, architectural lighting, control and custom-built elements into a refined architectural environment.

## Visual direction
Classical and elegant rather than cyberpunk. Architecture and detail lead; technology is integrated quietly.

### Materials
- warm timber / walnut
- glass
- dark metal
- concrete / stone
- vegetation

### Lighting
- dominant warm 2300K hospitality light
- 2300–2700K at selected bar layers
- restrained blue-violet ambient accent where supported by references
- no aggressive RGB aesthetic

### Graphics
- dark neutral background
- fine plan/elevation linework
- muted brass / warm gold callouts
- rectilinear annotation fields
- exploded axonometrics
- photo-to-plan transitions

## Motion grammar
1. real space
2. freeze / isolate
3. technical lines appear
4. components separate
5. plan / section / subsystem layer appears
6. components reassemble
7. clean final frame

## Public claim boundary
Generated boards and motion pieces are conceptual communication assets derived from project references. They do not replace verified construction drawings or commissioning records.
