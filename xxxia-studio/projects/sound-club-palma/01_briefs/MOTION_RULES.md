# MOTION RULES

- 16:9 master, 1920x1080 minimum.
- 24 or 25 fps.
- Calm motion; avoid fast gaming transitions.
- Exploded components separate on readable axes.
- Labels appear sequentially.
- Every sequence must have a clean 2–3 s hold frame for web use.
- Prefer 15–30 s per subsystem.
- Maintain a consistent 2300K warm-light reference.
- Finish with an archive-friendly poster frame.
